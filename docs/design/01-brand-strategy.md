## 1. Where we are

Guruphoria started in **2020** as an offline computer-education institute in
**Vikas Khand 5, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow**. It taught 200+ students in person,
moved online during the pandemic, published 63 videos and 21 live classes,
then paused.

**2026 = Chapter Two.** Guruphoria relaunches as an online, free, mentor-led
institute teaching **career skills + AI**.

### Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

These directly damage premium perception and must be removed:

- ❌ "10k+ subscribers" / "Join 10,000+ engineers" — channel has ~140 followers
- ❌ Topic tiles for **Kubernetes, Docker, Java, LLM Engineering, Cloud
  Computing, Firebase** — no content exists for these
- ❌ "Always Up To Date" while the library is 2020–21
- ❌ Presenting Vragger / Foundation as active

Replace with true, specific, stronger claims:

> **Est. 2020 · Lucknow · 200+ students taught · 5.0★ on Google · 100% free**

## 2. Positioning

> **Guruphoria — Build Your Essence**
>
> A learning institute born in a Lucknow classroom in 2020.
> We teach the complete professional: **technology, communication, and
> personality** — taught live by real mentors, in English and Hinglish.
> Completely free.

### Why this wins

There are thousands of AI/coding channels. There are almost none that teach
**tech + English communication + personality development** to Indian college
students, from a real institute with a real address and real reviews.

Our biggest differentiator is the thing the old site hid: **25 of our 63 videos
are Communication & Personality Development.** That is not side content — it is
what makes a student employable, and it is what makes Guruphoria different.

"Build Your Essence" finally means something concrete: we don't just teach
code, we build the whole person.

### Audience (two doors)

| Door | Who | Wants |
|---|---|---|
| **Student** | College / school, India-first | Placement readiness, projects, English, confidence |
| **Professional** | Working, upskilling | Practical web dev, Python/data, AI, modern tooling |

### Voice

Institutional, warm, plain-spoken. **"We" and "our mentors"**, never "I".
Specific over grand. No hype adjectives ("premier", "elite", "world-class").
Premium is demonstrated, never claimed.

## 4. Brand identity

### Colour tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `brand-900` | `#08325e` | Hero / footer background |
| `brand-700` | `#0d539b` | Primary — headings, brand surfaces |
| `brand-500` | `#0295d8` | Accent — links, interactive, highlights |
| `brand-100` | `#e6f4fd` | Tints, washes, soft backgrounds |
| `surface` | `#ffffff` / `#f7f9fc` | Content background |
| `live` | green | Reserved **only** for `FREE` / `LIVE` badges |

Both brand blues come from the existing logo. They are close in hue (210° /
199°), so hierarchy comes from the deepest logo blue and the light tint —
not from adding new hues.

### Theme: light-dominant editorial

White/near-white content body, deep-blue story band and footer for drama.

**Why not full dark:** the logo is blue-on-white; "Institute" trust reads
better light; 63 video thumbnails get visually noisy on dark; and dark +
gradient + glow is the generic 2023 AI-startup default we're trying to escape.

### Craft rules (what actually makes it premium)

1. Two typefaces maximum, strict modular scale, large headings, generous leading
2. One accent colour used **sparingly** — reduce glow/gradient by ~70%
3. Generous whitespace; vary section density deliberately
4. Hairline 1px low-opacity borders; one shadow system; consistent radii
5. Subtle, consistent motion — small staggered reveals, nothing bouncy
6. Everything aligned to one grid

**References:** Linear (restraint, motion), Stripe/Notion (editorial clarity),
Frontend Masters (program structure), Josh Comeau (solo-educator warmth).

### Tagline

**"Build Your Essence"** — retained from the original brand.

## 5. Site architecture

**Nav:** Programs · Mentors · Live · Story · Contact

Vragger and the Foundation live inside **Story**, in past tense, told with
pride — not in the nav.

### Homepage flow

1. **Hero** — Deepest logo blue, "Build Your Essence", honest stat strip
2. **Chapter Two** — short, confident relaunch note
3. **Two doors** — Student / Professional
4. **Programs** — 4 named tracks with module counts
5. **Mentors** — the section that ends the "portfolio" perception
6. **Our Story** — real offline photos, 2020 → pause → 2026 relaunch
7. **Proof** — Google/JustDial ratings + 10 testimonials with faces
8. **Archive** — 63 videos, clearly dated, organised
9. **Stay updated** — newsletter + YouTube subscribe
10. **Footer** — Lucknow roots, socials, honest legal

### Conversion priority (free model)

1. YouTube subscribe
2. Newsletter signup (the only audience we own)
3. Watch a video now
4. Contact / enquiry

The hero CTA is **"Watch Latest Class"** + **"Subscribe — Free"**, not
"Start Learning" pointing at a thin page.

## 6. Features worth building

- **Language badges + filter** — English / Hinglish as a first-class feature.
  A real differentiator for Indian learners; no competitor does this well.
- **Honest dating** — "Live Class · 2021" on archive content. Transparency
  reads premium; hidden dates read evasive.
- **Live duration badges** — "3h 12m live session". Nobody casually uploads
  three hours; length is a credibility signal.
- **Latest-from-Guruphoria strip** — auto-pulls newest upload. Always fresh,
  zero maintenance, works with an irregular publishing schedule.
- **Notify-me-when-live** capture — turns irregular scheduling into a reason
  to subscribe.
